## 1.Doro Wat... (Ethiopian Chicken Stew)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Sliced Red Onions
  2. Chopped Chicken (or Boiled Eggs)
  3. Berbere Spice Mix
  4. Niter Kibbeh (Spiced Clarified Butter)
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Reaching & Grabbing:** Pick up onions and chicken from the prep table and place them into the pot.
  * **Stirring (Wrist Motion):** Continuous circular wrist/arm movement to stir the stew while simmering.

## 2.Shiro Wat ... (Ethiopian Chickpea Stew)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Shiro Powder (Chickpea/Fava Bean Flour)
  2. Water or Warm Broth
  3. Chopped Garlic & Onions
  4. Cooking Oil / Niter Kibbeh
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Pinch & Release (Finger/Thumb Exercise):** Pinch a scoop of Shiro powder and release it over the boiling pot.
  * **Rapid Stirring (Wrist Rotation):** Smooth, repeated circular wrist movements to blend the powder and avoid lumps.

## 3.Ethiopian Salata. (Fresh Tomato & Onion Salad)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Diced Tomatoes
  2. Finely Chopped Onions
  3. Minced Jalapeño (Karia)
  4. Lemon Juice & Olive Oil Dressing
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Precision Pointing / Reaching:** Point finger to select and place individual salad vegetables into the bowl.
  * **Wrist Flexion / Tilting:** Tilt and turn the wrist to pour the salad dressing over the bowl.


## 4. Tomato and Egg Stir-fry (Scrambled Eggs with Tomato)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Sliced Tomatoes
  2. Beaten Eggs
  3. Sliced Green Onions
  4. Cooking Oil
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Reaching & Grabbing:** Pick tomatoes and egg bowl from the table and put them into the pan.
  * **Stirring (Wrist Motion):** Continuous circular wrist movement to stir eggs and tomatoes while frying.

## 5. Cucumber Salad
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Sliced Cucumber
  2. Minced Garlic
  3. Salt
  4. Vinegar Dressing
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Pinch & Release (Finger/Thumb Exercise):** Pinch garlic grains and salt, release them into the bowl of cucumber.
  * **Wrist Flexion / Tilting:** Tilt the wrist to pour vinegar dressing and toss the salad.

## 6. Steamed Tofu with Soy Sauce
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Tofu Blocks
  2. Chopped Spring Onion
  3. Soy Sauce
  4. A little Sesame Oil
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Precision Pointing / Reaching:** Point to select tofu and spring onion, place them on the plate.
  * **Slow Pouring (Wrist Rotation):** Gentle wrist rotation to pour soy sauce and sesame oil over tofu.
