## 1.Doro Wat... (Ethiopian Chicken Stew)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Sliced Red Onions
  2. Chopped Chicken (or Boiled Eggs)
  3. Berbere Spice Mix
  4. Niter Kibbeh (Spiced Clarified Butter)
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Reaching & Grabbing:** Pick up onions and chicken from the prep table and place them into the pot.
  * **Stirring (Wrist Motion):** Continuous circular wrist/arm movement to stir the stew while simmering.

## 2.Shiro Wat ... (Ethiopian Chickpea Stew)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Shiro Powder (Chickpea/Fava Bean Flour)
  2. Water or Warm Broth
  3. Chopped Garlic & Onions
  4. Cooking Oil / Niter Kibbeh
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Pinch & Release (Finger/Thumb Exercise):** Pinch a scoop of Shiro powder and release it over the boiling pot.
  * **Rapid Stirring (Wrist Rotation):** Smooth, repeated circular wrist movements to blend the powder and avoid lumps.

## 3.Ethiopian Salata. (Fresh Tomato & Onion Salad)
* **Ingredients:**
  1. Diced Tomatoes
  2. Finely Chopped Onions
  3. Minced Jalapeño (Karia)
  4. Lemon Juice & Olive Oil Dressing
* **Rehab Game Actions:**
  * **Precision Pointing / Reaching:** Point finger to select and place individual salad vegetables into the bowl.
  * **Wrist Flexion / Tilting:** Tilt and turn the wrist to pour the salad dressing over the bowl.
